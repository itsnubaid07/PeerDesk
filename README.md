# Peer Desk 

I made this so two people can chat, share files, talk, and draw together without creating an account or letting a company store our messages on a server.

Peer Desk connects your browser directly to your friend's browser. Nothing is saved online, and once you close the tab, the room is gone.

*Made by Nubaid*

👉 **Open Peer Desk:** (https://itsnubaid07.github.io/PeerDesk/)

## What You Can Do
* **Chat & Clean Links:** Send messages directly to your friend. Any link you paste is automatically cleaned of tracking tags (`utm_*`, `fbclid`, `si`) before sending.
* **Send Files:** Share files straight from your computer without uploading them anywhere first.
* **Voice Notes, Calls & Screen Share:** Record quick voice messages, start a live voice or video call, or share your screen.
* **Watch Together:** Load the same video file on both devices to play, pause, and skip together in sync.
* **Shared Sketchpad:** Draw math problems or quick notes together on a live board and save your sketch as a PNG image.
* **Chat Shortcuts:** Type `/help` in the chat box to see commands for clearing your screen (`/clear`), saving your notes (`/export`), or checking your connection speed (`/ping`).

## How to Connect
1. Click **Create Invite** (click **+ Voice**, **+ Video**, or **+ Screen** first if you want a live call) and copy the code to your friend.
2. Your friend pastes your code into the right box, clicks **Join Desk**, and sends their reply code back to you.
3. Paste their reply code into the right box and click **Complete Link**. Your desk is now connected.
